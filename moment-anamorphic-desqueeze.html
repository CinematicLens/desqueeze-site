Simple Moment steps

  1. Confirm Moment anamorphic (usually 1.33×).
  2. Open Anamorphic Desqueezer → choose 1.33× (Classic).
  3. Turn on live desqueeze + 2.39:1 guides if you need CinemaScope.
  4. Record, or batch-export after wrap (Web Studio works for quick tests).

Mixing lenses?

Split reels and tag the squeeze so post never applies the wrong preset. Other factors: use Custom or see the 1.33× guide and Get apps.

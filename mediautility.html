MediaUtility (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

  • Compress video with quality controls
  • Resize to 4K, 1080p, or 720p
  • Convert images to JPG or PNG
  • Private: everything stays on your device

MediaUtilityPro (timeline video editor)

App Store listing: a full timeline editor for iPhone, iPad, and Mac with multi-track clip arrangement, transitions, titles, captions, voiceover, music, filters, and one-tap HD export. Apple compatibility lists iOS / iPadOS 15+ and macOS 12+ on Apple silicon.

  • Timeline editing for arranging clips and refining pacing
  • Transitions, titles, stickers, and picture-in-picture overlays
  • Captions, voiceover recording, background music, fades, and smart ducking
  • Color filters, beat markers, HD export, and optional iCloud project access

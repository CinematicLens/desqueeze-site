Read the ring, not the marketing page

Sirui ships multiple squeeze factors even within one focal length family. Photograph the lens barrel or keep the factory sheet so you match decimals exactly. Between 1.5× and 1.8×? Use Custom and type the precise value.

Simple Sirui steps

  1. Note the squeeze on the Sirui ring (1.33× / 1.5× / 1.8× / 2×).
  2. Open Anamorphic Desqueezer on iPhone (or Web Studio for a quick test).
  3. Pick that preset — oval bokeh should look correct in preview.
  4. Live-record, or export CinemaScope / batch after wrap.

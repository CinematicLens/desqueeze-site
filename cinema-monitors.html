Workflow

Three steps from camera to monitored, de-squeezed image:

Step 1
Connect or shoot

Shoot on the device camera, or connect camera HDMI out to an HDMI-to-USB / UVC capture device, then to your Android phone or tablet via USB-C / OTG.

Step 2
Monitor & de-squeeze

Apply the anamorphic ratio, add framelines and safe areas, and check exposure and focus with zebra, false color, and peaking.

Step 3
Record or view clean

Record native anamorphic video, or switch to clean feed mode for a viewing-focused external monitor on set.

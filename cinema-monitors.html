Live External Monitor

Use your phone or tablet as a live external monitor for compatible USB / UVC camera workflows. Connect a supported HDMI-to-USB capture device and monitor the incoming feed with anamorphic de-squeeze, framing tools, exposure and focus aids, and manual rotate / flip controls.

  • Direct USB / UVC preview workflow
  • Manual monitor rotation controls
  • Flip horizontal / flip vertical controls
  • De-squeeze ratios for anamorphic monitoring
  • Framelines, safe area guides, grid and center mark
  • Zebra, false color, focus peaking
  • Clean feed mode for a viewing-focused external monitor workflow

External Monitor mode is designed for compatible USB / UVC capture workflows. Results depend on your phone or tablet, Android USB host support, capture device, power delivery, and the external camera output format.

Workflow

Three steps from camera to monitored, de-squeezed image:

Step 1
Connect or shoot

Shoot on the device camera, or connect camera HDMI out to an HDMI-to-USB / UVC capture device, then to your Android phone or tablet via USB-C / OTG.

Step 2
Monitor & de-squeeze

Apply the anamorphic ratio, add framelines and safe areas, and check exposure and focus with zebra, false color, and peaking.

Step 3
Record or view clean

Record native anamorphic video, or switch to clean feed mode for a viewing-focused external monitor on set.

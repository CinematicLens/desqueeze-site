Live-record anamorphic. Export CinemaScope. One app from capture through delivery.

Anamorphic Desqueezer is built for filmmakers who shoot anamorphic and need the take corrected while they roll — then handed to editorial without fixing geometry later.

LIVE RECORD
• Real-time desqueeze while you shoot — not a post-only fix
• Squeezed / desqueezed toggle
• 2.39:1 CinemaScope framing guides
• Record desqueezed; after a Pro take, the clip can save to Photos automatically
• USB-C SSD record on iOS 17+ (supported devices)

ON-SET SCOPES
• Zebras · IRE waveform · false color · focus peaking
• Luma / RGB histogram
• ISO, shutter, and white balance readouts

CINEMATIC CONTROLS (supported devices)
• Cinematic f-stop preview / capture on supported iPhones (iOS 26+)
• Color looks free to preview; baked into the take when you record with Pro
• Zoom and exposure choices carried into the exported movie

DELIVERY
• True 2.39:1 CinemaScope — no stretched footage
• ProRes and 10-bit export paths
• FCPXML for Final Cut so editorial inherits the same geometry
• Export to Photos or Files
• Batch clips · merge to one timeline · per-clip framing, LUT, trim
• Anamorphic photo / DNG stills

RATIOS
• 1.15× · 1.2× · 1.33× · 1.5× · 1.55× · 1.8× · 2.0× · Custom
• Full manual control — no automatic detection

PLATFORMS
• iPhone · iPad · Mac (Universal Purchase; Mac is post-focused)
• Product page: https://anamorphic-desqueeze.com/anamorphic-desqueeze-iphone.html

Preview ratios, f-stops, and looks free. Unlock Pro to record and export (Photos, Files, batch, merge, ProRes / 10-bit, FCPXML).

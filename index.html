How it works

Anamorphic lenses squeeze the image horizontally; we restore the correct aspect ratio (e.g. 2.39:1). Choose your factor (1.33×–2× or custom), then export. Use the browser for quick batches, or our iOS, Android (demo), or Windows apps for larger projects.

Built for indie filmmakers & anamorphic shooters in the USA & Canada

From indie features and short films to music videos, commercials, weddings, and YouTube channels, creators across the United States and Canada use DeSqueeze Studio to finish footage with a clean cinematic frame. Whether you shoot in Los Angeles, New York, Atlanta, Austin, Chicago, Toronto, Vancouver, or Montreal, the workflow is the same: de-squeeze, grade, and export — no region lock and no subscription required to start.

It fits the gear indie filmmakers actually use in North America. De-squeeze 1.33×, 1.5×, 1.8×, and 2× footage from popular anamorphic adapters and lenses like Moment, SIRUI, Moondog Labs, Atlas Orion, Vazen, and Great Joy, shot on iPhone, Android, mirrorless, or cinema cameras. Source frame rates are preserved, including the NTSC standards common in the US and Canada (23.976, 29.97, and 59.94 fps).

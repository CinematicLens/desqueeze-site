CineMonitor

CineMonitor is the web name for HDMI Monitor - Cine on the App Store — a professional iPad cinema and field monitor for filmmakers, camera assistants, DITs, and colorists. Connect camera HDMI through a UVC capture card to USB-C iPad Pro or iPad Air and monitor live with low-latency GPU tools built for set.

Live HDMI monitoring on iPad

  • Capture: external HDMI/UVC live preview, resolution/FPS readout, hot-plug reconnect, audio meters
  • Focus & exposure: peaking, zebra, false color, 1:1 / 2× / 4× zoom loupe
  • Scopes: luma waveform, histogram, RGB parade, vectorscope — overlay or dedicated workspace
  • Color: import industry-standard .cube 3D LUTs, intensity control, instant A/B bypass
  • Composition: 1.85:1, 2.39:1, 4:3, 9:16, custom guides; still capture to Photos

Requires a compatible HDMI/UVC capture device. Designed for landscape iPad Pro and iPad Air with USB-C. General-purpose monitor — no anamorphic desqueeze (see CineMonitors on Android or Anamorphic Desqueezer for desqueeze workflows).

Who it is for

On-set monitoring when you want iPad portability with cinema-grade overlays — without lugging a full SmallHD or TVLogic rig. Pairs naturally with CineLut Live Grade on Mac for graded dailies, and with ACamViewfinder for focus/diopter prep on iPhone and iPad.

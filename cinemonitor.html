CineMonitor

CineMonitor is the web name for HDMI Monitor - Cine on the Apple App Store — a professional iPad cinema and field monitor for filmmakers, camera assistants, DITs, and colorists. Connect camera HDMI through a UVC capture card to USB-C iPad Pro or iPad Air and monitor live with low-latency GPU tools built for set.

What CineMonitor gives you

Camera HDMI → UVC capture → iPad. Professional overlays without a dedicated monitor cart.

Scopes on set

Waveform, histogram, RGB parade, vectorscope — overlay or dedicated workspace.

.cube LUT library

Import industry LUTs, intensity control, instant A/B bypass vs clean preview.

  • CaptureHDMI / UVC live preview · resolution / FPS · hot-plug · audio meters

  • Frame1.85:1 · 2.39:1 · 4:3 · 9:16 · custom · stills to Photos

Who it is for

Camera assistants, DITs, and directors who want iPad portability with cinema overlays — without a full SmallHD / TVLogic cart for every setup. Pairs with CineLut Live Grade on Mac for graded dailies, and ACamViewfinder for focus prep.

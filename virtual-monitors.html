What is Virtual Monitors?

Virtual Monitors is a workflow product page for running extra screens virtually, so you can keep your main editing canvas clean while still seeing the information you need.

  • Keep one screen for the timeline and another for notes, references, or chat
  • Separate preview/review from UI panels
  • Maintain a consistent layout while switching between pages on this site

Common use cases

  • Editing: timeline on the main display, bins/notes on a secondary virtual monitor
  • Review: keep a clean preview area while you write feedback or compare stills
  • On-set / desk monitoring: reference frames, LUT notes, camera reports, shot lists
  • Delivery: export queue on one screen while you prep versions and uploads on another

CineMonitor (iPad) — App Store

CineMonitor is our name for HDMI Monitor - Cine on the App Store — a professional iPad cinema and field monitor for filmmakers, camera assistants, DITs, and colorists. Full product page: CineMonitor overview.

  • Capture: external HDMI/UVC live preview, resolution/FPS readout, hot-plug reconnect, audio meters
  • Focus & exposure: peaking, zebra, false color, 1:1 / 2× / 4× zoom loupe
  • Scopes: luma waveform, histogram, RGB parade, vectorscope — overlay or dedicated workspace
  • Color: import .cube 3D LUTs, intensity control, instant A/B bypass
  • Composition: 1.85:1, 2.39:1, 4:3, 9:16, custom guides; still capture to Photos

Cinema Monitor (Android) — Live external monitor

From the Google Play listing, Cinema Monitor is built for two pro workflows: native anamorphic recording and live external monitoring. Use a compatible HDMI-to-USB / UVC capture device to preview a camera feed on your phone/tablet, with de-squeeze and monitoring tools.

  • Live External Monitor: USB / UVC preview, manual rotate, flip horizontal/vertical
  • Anamorphic de-squeeze ratios: 1.00×, 1.33×, 1.50×, 1.55×, 1.80×, 2.00×
  • Monitoring overlays: framelines, safe area guides, grid/center mark
  • Exposure + focus aids: zebra, false color, focus peaking
  • Clean feed mode for viewing-focused monitoring

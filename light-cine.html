Why crews reach for Light Cine

  • Cinema-first metering — not a consumer photo EV toy. Relative exposure guidance tied to how you shoot: FPS, shutter angle, ISO/EI, aperture, ND, and compensation.
  • Scopes you trust on set — false color, histogram, waveform, adjustable zebras, highlight and shadow percentiles.
  • Lighting continuity — sample key and fill, read stop difference and ratio, then use Exposure Match when you move to the next setup.
  • Subject-aware sampling — on-device face-region hints (regions only, no identity) with manual region fallback when you need control.
  • Projects that travel with the day — shots, notes, and exports to PDF, CSV, or JSON for prep and handoff.
  • Built-in cinema calculators — exposure, shutter angle, ND, frame-rate compensation, inverse square, depth of field, and field of view in one kit.

Who it is for

Directors of photography, gaffers, camera operators, indie filmmakers, and creators who want fast, private guidance while blocking light — without signing into another cloud service.

Privacy by default (good for set)

No account. No tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs. Camera frames stay on your device; reference stills live in your projects until you export them.

Note: Light Cine gives relative on-device guidance. It is not a certified lux meter and does not replace calibrated professional meters — use it to move faster between setups, then verify with your house standards.

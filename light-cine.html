What Light Cine does — clear features

Answers one set question: what should I change to expose and light this shot correctly?

  • MeterCinema units — FPS · shutter angle · ISO/EI · T-stop · ND · compensation
  • ScopesFalse color · histogram · waveform · adjustable zebras
  • LightingKey / fill ratio · stop difference · Exposure Match between setups
  • CalcExposure · ND · inverse square · DOF · FOV in one kit
  • ProjectsShots · notes · export PDF / CSV / JSON
  • PrivacyNo account · on device · no ad SDKs

Who it is for

DPs, gaffers, operators, and indie filmmakers who want fast, private guidance while blocking light — without another cloud login.

Privacy by default (good for set)

No account. No tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs. Camera frames stay on your device; reference stills live in your projects until you export them.

Note: Light Cine gives relative on-device guidance. It is not a certified lux meter and does not replace calibrated professional meters — use it to move faster between setups, then verify with your house standards.

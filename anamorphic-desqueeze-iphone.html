How to use it (simple)

  1. Put your anamorphic lens on iPhone / iPad and open Anamorphic Desqueezer.
  2. Pick the squeeze printed on the lens (or Custom).
  3. Turn on live desqueeze + scopes. Frame 2.39:1 if you need CinemaScope.
  4. Record. Unlock Pro to export ProRes / 10-bit, FCPXML, and batch.

Anamorphic flow

This lane treats anamorphic as its own pipeline: ratio math, monitoring, batch correction, and export. Start in the free Web Studio for 1.33×–2× previews and batch ×10 MP4/JPG/PNG.

On iPhone, iPad, and MacBook, Anamorphic Desqueezer is the native flagship: live monitor and post, ProRes and 10-bit paths, scopes, and FCPXML so editorial inherits the same geometry. FilmStudio stays the capture-side companion when you are framing widescreen in-camera.

Android (Google Play): Anamorphic Desqueezer for batch video and stills, Cinemascope 2.39:1 up to 4K, LUT pipeline, and LiveGrade-style monitoring with 3D LUT + CDL. Anamorphic Desqueeze Pro Cam for live de-squeeze on set.

Windows: AnamorphicDesqueezePro for folder-based batch de-squeeze, CinemaScope presets, LUT/CDL, and EDL-friendly exports.

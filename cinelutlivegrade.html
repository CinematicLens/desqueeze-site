Wireless & HDMI / SDI camera monitoring

CineLut Live Grade supports wireless camera monitoring workflows using HDMI/SDI wireless transmitters and macOS-compatible capture devices—the same live LUT preview, CDL-style grading, scopes, and ProRes recording you get on a hard-wired feed.

Connect your camera to a wireless video transmitter, send the signal to a receiver, plug the receiver into your Mac through an HDMI or SDI capture device, and grade in CineLut Live Grade. What works in practice depends on the camera body, output format, transmitter/receiver pair, and capture card you use on high-end productions.

Typical workflow: Camera → Wireless transmitter → Receiver → Capture card → MacBook → CineLut Live Grade

Compatible with cinema cameras such as ARRI, RED, Blackmagic, Sony, Canon, Panasonic GH/GH4/GH5/GH6, Lumix S-series, Nikon, Fujifilm, and other bodies that output clean HDMI/SDI—when paired with the right wireless and capture hardware.

  • Direct camera Wi‑Fi varies by model; many high-end bodies are built for HDMI/SDI out, not app-level Wi‑Fi monitoring.
  • Professional low-latency monitoring is usually best with dedicated HDMI/SDI wireless systems plus a low-latency capture device—not camera Wi‑Fi alone.
  • Wireless latency is typically about 0.06s–0.5s, depending on transmitter, receiver, capture card, camera output, and network conditions.

Note: CineLut Live Grade works with the signal your hardware delivers. Confirm HDMI/SDI output, resolution, and timecode support on your camera and capture card before shoot day.

Post handoff

Graded ProRes is only half the story: CineLutLiveGrade packages CDL, AMF, LUT, and EDL+CDL so Silverstack, DaVinci Resolve, and editorial can re-link the same look without guesswork. Shot stills and per-scene looks stay in the library for continuity across setups.

Who it’s for

Cinematographers, DOPs, DITs, colorists, and film crews who need to see the final look while the camera is rolling. Built for professional productions and independent filmmakers everywhere. Apply LUTs live while shooting, monitor 2.39:1 framing, and record directly to MacBook storage. For live ingest, use a compatible SDI/HDMI capture device—including feeds from wireless transmitter/receiver chains when your camera and hardware support it—then export graded ProRes clips ready for editorial, dailies, and cloud upload workflows.

What you get

  • Live grading — 3D LUTs and ASC-CDL-style primaries (slope, offset, power, saturation), with 2.39:1 monitoring support. Waveform, parade, histogram, vectorscope on every frame.
  • Recording — Graded ProRes with optional burn-in (timecode, reel/scene/take, slate).
  • Dailies & handoff — Export graded ProRes, CDL, AMF, LUT files. EDL + CDL packages for Silverstack, DaVinci Resolve, and other post tools.
  • Shot library — Save stills and looks per scene. Import/export CDL and LUT. Presets and consistent looks across takes.

Why on-set matters

Unlike offline grading, CineLutLiveGrade is built for live work. You see what the camera sees, with your look applied. What you record is what the editor sees. Looks stay consistent across takes and the pipeline from set to dailies stays predictable.
